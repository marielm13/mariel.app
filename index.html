AI and automation

Applying AI in organizations that weren't built for it.

Most of my AI work happens in teams that aren't tech-native. I build tools for my own work first, then teach my team to build theirs.

Flagship

Personalized attestation reports for every member school

The problem
CAPS didn't show attestation status, and the senior leaders accountable for attestation don't work in CAPS day to day.
What I built
Used Claude Cowork and the terminal to design and generate a personalized report for each member school, then automated delivery to the correct account holders.
What changed
Every two weeks for eight weeks, 319 schools and 35 conferences got a personalized progress report, reaching 1,000+ school and 100+ conference leaders. Nearly all 319 schools completed attestation (96%).
How I made sure it was right
Random spot checks on report data and recipients before each send, plus open tracking to confirm reports reached the right leaders.

How I work

Adaptability

A good PM can be dropped into any team or industry. Execution, judgment, curiosity, and customer obsession travel everywhere. Too much weight on subject-matter expertise can bring fixed habits that crowd out creative problem solving.

Curiosity

I stay curious about my industry and the world beyond it. The inspiration for solving a problem often comes from unexpected places and people, and you only find it without blinders on.

Customer obsession

My job is to be the customer's voice, whether they're buying a book or building a roster. Sometimes that means building things that don't directly feed the bottom line. When people feel heard and friction disappears, loyalty follows.
