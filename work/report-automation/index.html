Personalized attestation reports for every member school

Ahead of the first year-end attestation, schools and conferences needed to see exactly where they stood. I used Claude Cowork and the terminal to design and generate a personalized report for each member school, then automated delivery to the correct account holders.

Claude CoworkTerminalAutomated delivery

The problem

Attestation was a first-time, high-stakes process. Schools needed to know which of their revenue share, scholarship, and roster data still needed attention, and conferences needed to see how their member schools were progressing.

todo: how schools would have tracked progress without these reports

What I built

  1. Design the reportWorked with Claude to design a report layout that shows each school its own data and progress toward attestation.
  2. Generate one per schoolUsed Claude Cowork and the terminal to produce a personalized report for each member school.
  3. Route to the right peopleAutomated delivery so each report went to that school's correct account holders.
  4. Track progressSchools and conferences used the reports to follow their progress toward year-end attestation.

How I made sure it was right

Each report held one school's compliance data, so accuracy and delivery to the right people mattered as much as speed.
